About Scuderia Ferrari

Scuderia Ferrari HP is the oldest and most successful team in Formula 1 history. Founded by Enzo Ferrari in 1929, the team has been competing in the FIA Formula One World Championship since its inaugural season in 1950. Based in Maranello, Italy, Ferrari represents passion, speed, and excellence on every circuit around the world.

  • Founded in 1929 by the legendary Enzo Ferrari in Maranello, Italy.
  • The only team to have competed in every Formula 1 World Championship season.
  • Officially known as Scuderia Ferrari HP since their partnership with HP in 2024.

Ferrari's legacy is defined by iconic red cars, record-breaking championships, and legendary drivers. With 16 Constructors' titles and 15 Drivers' titles, the Scuderia remains the benchmark of Formula 1 excellence. The passionate fanbase — the Tifosi — are among the most devoted supporters in all of motorsport, filling grandstands at every race with a sea of red.

The Ferrari SF-26 — 2026 Formula 1 Car

The SF-26 is Ferrari's challenger for the 2026 Formula 1 season. Engineered at Maranello with cutting-edge aerodynamics and Ferrari's latest power unit, the SF-25 embodies decades of racing DNA combined with modern innovation.

01 What engine does the SF-26 use?

The SF-26 is powered by Ferrari's 066/16 turbocharged 1.6L V6 hybrid power unit, delivering over 1,000 horsepower combined with the MGU-H and MGU-K energy recovery systems.

02 What are the key aerodynamic updates for 2026?

The SF-26 features a revised front wing, updated sidepod concept, and an optimised rear diffuser to improve downforce and reduce drag on high-speed circuits. The Scuderia invested heavily in wind tunnel and CFD simulation work during the off-season.

03 What is the SF-26's livery and colour scheme?

The SF-26 carries the iconic Scuderia Ferrari red livery with HP branding as the title sponsor. The car features the prancing horse badge and a bold red-and-black colour scheme inspired by Ferrari's racing heritage.

Charles Leclerc

Driver #16 · Monaco 🇲🇨

Born in Monaco in 1997, Charles Leclerc joined Ferrari's driver academy in 2016 and became an F1 race driver in 2018. Known for his exceptional one-lap pace and spectacular qualifying performances, Leclerc is one of the most naturally gifted drivers of his generation.

Lewis Hamilton

Driver #44 · United Kingdom 🇬🇧

7-time Formula 1 World Champion Lewis Hamilton joined Scuderia Ferrari for the 2025 season, in one of the most eagerly anticipated moves in the sport's history. With 104 race victories, Hamilton brings unrivalled experience and championship pedigree to the Maranello team.
